# License texts

Complete license texts for the third-party components listed in
[../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). The identifiers used there are SPDX identifiers.

The texts are unmodified copies of the licenses published by their authors. They do not replace copyright notices
that are part of the corresponding source distributions; the component table and upstream source links in the
notices identify those distributions.

The AppImage runtime's own notice is in [AppImage-runtime-LICENSE.txt](AppImage-runtime-LICENSE.txt). The
freedesktop sound theme's credits are in
[sound-theme-freedesktop-CREDITS.txt](sound-theme-freedesktop-CREDITS.txt).
